The pilot workspace says which study it is, and the simulated field work stops clustering at one end.

Two changes, both about a workspace that should feel like the real project it is built on:

**The Command Center names the study.** The project record has carried the terms of reference's own
title and the programme it belongs to since the cartography was imported, and nothing showed them.
The header now does — quietly, under the short name the team uses in conversation, because it is the
name the deliverable will be filed under.

**The demonstration campaign is spread along the corridor.** Its twelve assignments were the first
twelve parcels by chainage, which was invisible while the corridor was synthetic and, with the
study's real chainages, put every simulated visit in the first few hundred metres. The map showed a
demonstration that had apparently worked one end of the road and stopped. They are now every
*n*-th parcel in chainage order, from abscissa 0 to the end of the alignment, still deterministic
(TD-071).
